Account (abstract base)
├── account_number, owner, _balance
├── @property balance
├── deposit() - concrete, same for all
├── withdraw() - abstract, different per account type
├── __str__, __repr__

SavingsAccount(Account)
├── interest_rate
├── withdraw() - cannot go below minimum balance
├── add_interest() - adds interest to balance

CurrentAccount(Account)
├── overdraft_limit
├── withdraw() - can go negative up to overdraft limit

Bank (composition)
├── name
├── accounts (list)
├── add_account(account)
├── find_account(account_number)
├── total_deposits()
├── show_all_accounts()
